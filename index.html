1. Share of Voice

Each brand's share of all unique articles published. LATAM consolidated — Muck Rack alerts do not break down by country.

2. Trend over time

Share of Voice month by month. The month is the date of the processed alert.

3. Movement alerts

The most recent month compared with the average of previous months.

4. Topics and narratives

What each brand is mentioned for. Classified by keywords in the headline, in Portuguese, Spanish and English — this is an approximation: one headline can fall under more than one topic, and anything matching no keyword shows up as “no topic identified”.

5. Coverage tone

Sentiment as classified by Muck Rack. The percentage counts only the articles that came in classified.

6. Outlets

Who publishes about the industry, and which brands each outlet covers.

Data collected from Muck Rack alerts, processed automatically on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

This dashboard measures volume of coverage, not editorial quality. Duplicates flagged by Muck Rack are discarded.
